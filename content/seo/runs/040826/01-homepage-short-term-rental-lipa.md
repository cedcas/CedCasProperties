## Why Lipa City for Your Next Getaway?

If you haven't been to Lipa recently, you're sleeping on one of Batangas' best-kept secrets. Here's why more travelers are choosing Lipa over the usual weekend spots:

**It's crazy close to Manila.** We're talking about one hour via SLEX and STAR Tollway on a good day. Even on a Friday afternoon, you're looking at maybe an hour and a half. Compare that to the 3-4 hour crawl to La Union or Baguio and suddenly Lipa looks pretty appealing, right?

**The climate is noticeably cooler.** Lipa sits on a highland plateau, which means the air here is fresher and cooler than Metro Manila — especially in the mornings and evenings. You might not even need the aircon some nights. (But don't worry, we have aircon.)

**It's the gateway to everything.** Mt. Maculot — one of the most popular day hikes in the Philippines — is a 20-minute drive away. Taal Volcano and Taal Heritage Town are just down the road. Masasa Beach is doable as a day trip. And Lipa itself has incredible coffee, great local food, and enough malls and restaurants that you won't feel like you're in the middle of nowhere.

Whether you're hiking, exploring, celebrating, or just escaping the city for 48 hours, Lipa is the sweet spot.
