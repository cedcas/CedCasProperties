## What to Do After Work Hours

One of the best parts about a remote work staycation is that you're not stuck in the same four walls every single day. Lipa City has plenty to offer once you log off:

**Sunset hike at Mt. Maculot.** Just a short drive from town, Mt. Maculot is one of the most popular day hikes in Batangas. The Rockies viewpoint gives you an insane panoramic view — perfect for golden hour photos. Do this on a weekend morning or late afternoon and you'll feel like a completely different person by Monday.

**Coffee run at Café de Lipa.** Lipa is literally the coffee capital of Batangas. Café de Lipa serves local Barako coffee and kapeng barako blends that'll put your overpriced Manila flat white to shame. Grab a bag of beans to take home while you're at it.

**Food trip around town.** From local goto and bulalo spots to surprisingly good cafés and restaurants, Lipa's food scene is underrated. Everything is affordable too — we're talking ₱100-200 meals that are actually filling.

**Explore local culture.** Check out San Sebastian Cathedral, stroll through the old houses in the heritage district, or visit the Lipa City public market for the full local experience. It's a nice way to disconnect from screens and reconnect with, well, real life.

## Digital Nomad Visa: Work Legally From the Philippines

Here's a game-changer for foreign remote workers: the Philippines officially launched its **Digital Nomad Visa in 2025**. This allows foreign nationals earning income from outside the country to live and work in the Philippines for up to **two years**.

Requirements include proof of remote employment or freelance income, health insurance, and a minimum income threshold. If you're a foreign digital nomad who's been eyeing Southeast Asia, this makes the Philippines — and affordable spots like Lipa City — seriously attractive.

Filipino remote workers, you obviously don't need a visa. But you *do* need a break from Manila. Just saying.

## How to Get Here From Manila

Getting to Lipa is easier than you think:

**By car:** Take SLEX to STAR Tollway, exit at Lipa. Total drive time is about **1 hour** with light traffic. Toll fees run around ₱200-300. Parking at HavenInLipa is free.

**By bus:** From PITX, take an ALPS bus headed to Lipa — it's about **2 hours** and costs around **₱238**. JAM Liner from Buendia is another option at ₱150-200.

**By Grab or taxi:** Expect to pay around ₱2,500-3,000. Makes sense if you're splitting with a group.
