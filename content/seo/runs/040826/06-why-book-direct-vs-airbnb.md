## How Much Do Airbnb Fees Really Cost You?

Most guests don't realize how much Airbnb adds on top of the listed nightly rate. The platform charges a **service fee of 14–20%** to guests, and that's before cleaning fees or any other extras.

Let's look at real numbers using our Spacious 2BR Getaway as an example:

| | Book Direct | Book via Airbnb |
|---|---|---|
| Nightly Rate | ₱2,800 | ₱2,800 |
| Airbnb Service Fee (14%) | — | ₱392 |
| 2-Night Stay Base | ₱5,600 | ₱5,600 |
| Total Airbnb Fees | ₱0 | ₱784 |
| **Total You Pay** | **₱5,600** | **₱6,384** |
| **Your Savings (Direct)** | **₱784** | — |

For a 2-night stay, that's almost ₱800 you could spend on a Taal Lake boat tour, a nice dinner in Lipa, or a few rounds of coffee at Café de Lipa. For longer stays — say 4 or 5 nights — the savings get even bigger.

And here's what most people don't know: Airbnb also takes a **3% commission from hosts**. So on a ₱5,600 booking, they take ₱168 from us too. Everyone pays, and the platform keeps both cuts.

Now let's see the same comparison for our Cozy 1BR Haven:

| | Book Direct | Book via Airbnb |
|---|---|---|
| Nightly Rate | ₱2,000 | ₱2,000 |
| Airbnb Service Fee (14%) | — | ₱280 |
| 2-Night Stay Base | ₱4,000 | ₱4,000 |
| Total Airbnb Fees | ₱0 | ₱560 |
| **Total You Pay** | **₱4,000** | **₱4,560** |
| **Your Savings (Direct)** | **₱560** | — |

No hidden math. No surprises. That's just how the fees work.

## Is It Safe to Book Direct?

This is the most important question, and you should absolutely ask it. Not every "book direct" offer is legitimate, and being cautious is smart.

Here's how to verify any direct booking property — and here's why HavenInLipa checks every box:

**How to verify legitimacy:**

- **Check for real reviews.** We have **51+ verified guest reviews** across platforms. Real guests, real feedback, real photos.
- **Look for social media presence.** Find us on Facebook and Instagram with actual guest photos, not stock images. Our pages are active, updated, and full of real interactions.
- **Confirm business registration.** HavenInLipa is **DTI-registered** — a legitimate, compliant business in the Philippines. You can ask us for our registration details anytime.
- **Video call or message first.** We're happy to do a quick video call or chat before you book. If a host refuses to show you the property or talk to you, that's a red flag. We're the opposite — we want you to feel confident.
- **Check Google Maps and listings.** Our properties show up on Google with reviews, photos, and a verified location in Lipa City.

**Red flags to watch for with other properties:**

- No reviews or social media presence
- Refuses to provide ID or registration
- Only accepts Western Union or untraceable payment
- Pressures you to pay immediately with no confirmation
- Photos look too polished or generic

With HavenInLipa, what you see is what you get. We've built our reputation on honesty, and we're not about to risk it.
