# Conversion Fix #4 — Email Capture / Lead Magnet
**HavenInLipa · Delta run 070426 · Priority: 🟠 MEDIUM (compounds over time)**

## The problem
Your blog has **no email capture anywhere** (confirmed on the live #1 article). Most organic readers aren't ready to book *today* — they're planning a trip weeks out. Without a capture + nurture path, **100% of not-ready-yet readers are lost.** Even a modest 2–3% opt-in on growing traffic builds a list of warm, pre-qualified Lipa travelers you can convert on your schedule, not theirs.

## The lead magnet (pick one to start)
**Recommended: "The Lipa Weekend Planner"** — a free, genuinely useful PDF: a ready-to-use 2-day and 3-day Lipa itinerary (built straight from your existing articles — things to do, food, Taal, Mt. Maculot), with a subtle "where to stay" page featuring your properties + the book-direct savings. It matches your parent-to-parent voice and your top-of-funnel traffic perfectly.

**Alternative / add-on: a first-booking incentive** — "Planning a Lipa trip? Get **₱[X] off your first direct booking** + our free weekend planner." Ties the capture directly to a booking nudge and reinforces the book-direct angle.

## Placement (highest-converting spots)
| Spot | Form | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Inline, after the intro** | 1-field (email) + "Send me the planner" | Catches engaged readers early |
| **End of article** | Same | Natural "what next" after they've read |
| **Exit-intent popup** | Same, dismissible | Recovers leavers without annoying readers |
| **Sidebar (desktop)** | Persistent | Always available |

Keep it **one field (email only)** — every extra field cuts opt-ins.

## Opt-in copy (on-brand, honest)
> **Planning a Lipa trip?** Grab our free **Lipa Weekend Planner** — a locals' 2-and-3-day itinerary (where to eat, what to skip, how to beat the Tagaytay crowds). No spam, just the good stuff.
> **[email field]** → **Send me the planner**

## Nurture sequence (4 emails, automated)
1. **Instant — deliver the planner.** "Here's your planner + a quick tip most first-timers miss." Soft intro to Haven in Lipa.
2. **Day 3 — value.** "The 3 things guests wish they'd known about Lipa." Genuinely helpful; one contextual property mention.
3. **Day 6 — the book-direct case.** "Why we ask you to book direct (and how you save 15–20%)." Link property pages + the #6 Book-Direct article.
4. **Day 10 — soft offer / seasonality.** "Weekends fill up around long weekends — here's what's open." Link availability; include the incentive if you're running one.
