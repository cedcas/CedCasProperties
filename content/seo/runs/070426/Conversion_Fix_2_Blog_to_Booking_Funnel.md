# Conversion Fix #2 — Blog → Booking Funnel Remap
**HavenInLipa · Delta run 070426 · Priority: 🔴 HIGH**

## The problem (found on the live site, 2026-07-04)
Your best organic entry page — **#1 "15 Best Things to Do in Lipa"** — behaves like a **dead-end info page**, not a funnel:
- Booking links appear only **~60% down** the article (below most readers' scroll).
- It links only to the **old Cozy 1BR / Spacious 2BR** — **not the new Mickey family houses.** A parent reading "things to do with kids" never sees your Disney family house.
- CTAs are **informational, not persuasive** ("Book your stay at HavenInLipa →").
- **No email capture** — every reader who isn't ready to book today is lost forever (see [Conversion Fix #4](Conversion_Fix_4_Email_Capture_Lead_Magnet.md)).

The content engine is doing its job (traffic ↑). The **hand-off to booking is where it leaks.**

## Two universal rules (apply to every article)
1. **Move a property callout ABOVE the fold of value** — insert a contextual "Where to stay" callout box **right after the intro / first H2**, not buried at the end. Keep the end-of-article CTA too.
2. **Match the property to the article's intent** — link the unit a reader of *that* article would actually book. Stop defaulting every article to 1BR/2BR.
