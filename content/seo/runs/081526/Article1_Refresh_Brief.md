## The edits

### 1. Sharpen the head term 🔴

The exact phrase **`things to do in Lipa City`** must appear:

- **Leading the H1** — first four words, not buried behind "15 Best"
- In the **title tag**, as early as the format allows
- In the **first 100 words** of body copy, naturally
- As **one H2**, verbatim

Suggested H1: **Things to Do in Lipa City: 15 Places a Local Actually Sends People**

Keeps the listicle promise and the local-authority angle, but leads with the term. Do not change the slug.

### 2. Add an H2 for the kids intent 🔴

`where to go in lipa with kids` sits at **10.78** with no dedicated section anywhere on the site. It is the closest informational query in the whole dataset to a booking — a family searching this is one step from needing a house that sleeps seven.

**New H2: "Things to do in Lipa City with kids"**

Pull the family-suitable entries already in the article into one place, add which are stroller-friendly and which need a car, and close with a link to the **Mickey houses** — Disney-themed rooms are a genuinely strong answer to "what do I do with the kids in Lipa."

### 3. Add a one-day H2 🟠

**New H2: "Where to go in Lipa if you only have one day"**

Captures the `where to go` variants (16 impressions at 11.06) and serves a real reader need the current structure ignores: a ranked shortlist rather than fifteen equal options. Three or four picks in a sensible driving order.

### 4. Add local proof to each entry 🟠

The differentiator against TripAdvisor and Traveloka is that **none of their writers have been there**. Per entry, add:

- **Drive time from the city centre** ⚠️ *verify — don't estimate*
- **Best time to go** — "before 9am or you'll queue"
- **Current entrance fee**, or "free" ⚠️ *verify*

This is what a local guide has and an aggregator can't fake.

The article currently ends with the five-property ladder. Once `/properties` is live, point **"Book direct and save ›"** at `https://haveninlipa.com/properties` instead of the interim `/#properties` anchor.
