## The situation

Climbing Mt. Maculot is **strictly prohibited** — all areas, all activities, any purpose. The Cuenca tourism office reaffirmed this in a **July 2026** advisory and explicitly denied claims that other sections are open or that only the Rockies and Summit are closed. It is enforced under **Executive Order No. 1, Series of 2020**, for rehabilitation and reforestation. No confirmed reopening date; a date has been floated before and did not hold.

**Separately:** the drive from our houses to the jump-off is about **50 minutes** (owner, 2026-08-16). Our content says **15**.

### Why this is the top of the queue

Article #4 published **13 April 2026** — the mountain was already closed under a 2020 order. The article has spent four months explaining how to climb it and inviting people to book with us as a base camp. The failure mode is concrete: a guest books *because* of #4, drives down for a 5:15 AM start, and finds a 50-minute drive to a mountain they are not permitted to climb.

This is a trust problem before it is an SEO problem. Fix it first.
