# HavenInLipa Blog Posting Guide
### For: Articles 11 & 12 (Family Staycation + Romantic Getaway)
### Instructions for Blogger

Use this guide when posting each article to WordPress. For each article, set the metadata in the Post sidebar, add the tags, select the category, upload the featured image, and place images in the correct sections.

**Reminder:** In the article files, `##` = H2 heading, `###` = H3 heading. The title (H1) is already set in the WordPress Title field. Never add another H1 inside the content.

**Special note for these two articles:** Both are lifestyle / use-case pieces aimed at distinct audiences — Article #11 is for Manila parents planning a kid-friendly weekend, Article #12 is for couples looking to skip Tagaytay. Image tone should match: Article #11 warm, busy, family-energy; Article #12 quiet, slow, two-people-only. Avoid using the same stock photo across both.

## Tone & Photo Direction — Critical Distinction

These two articles target opposite audiences. Keep the visual languages clearly separated:

| Cue | Article #11 (Family) | Article #12 (Couples) |
|---|---|---|
| People in frame | Two adults + kids, often four people | Two adults only, no kids ever |
| Mood | Warm, busy, daylight | Quiet, slow, evening / golden hour |
| Color tone | Brighter, more saturated | Softer, warmer, more muted |
| Settings to favor | Garden seating, playgrounds, kitchen with kids' items | Candlelit tables, sunset viewpoints, intimate interiors |
| Settings to avoid | Anything that looks staged for a couple's date | Anything with kids, toys, high chairs, or family chaos |

**Do NOT cross-use images between the two articles.** Each photo should belong unmistakably to one audience. The Casa Marikit, Cafe de Lipa, and Taal Heritage Town shots in particular need different framings between the two posts — even if shot on the same scouting trip, choose deliberately different angles per article.

## How to Add Images in WordPress

1. Click where you want the image in your content
2. Type `/image` and press Enter
3. Click **Upload** and select your file
4. After uploading, click the image → go to **Block** tab in right sidebar → fill in **Alt Text**
5. Keep image file names lowercase with hyphens (e.g., `haveninlipa-1br-couples-bedroom.jpg`)
6. For restaurant photos sourced from a brand's Facebook page, add a small caption underneath crediting the source (e.g., "Photo: Casa Marikit") — protects you and is good practice.

## Featured Image Setup (Both Articles)

In the WordPress Post sidebar:
1. Click **Featured Image** → **Set Featured Image**
2. Upload the featured image file
3. Fill in Alt Text (use the **Featured Image Alt Text** from each article's metadata above)
4. Save

The featured image is what shows on the blog homepage and on social shares — pick the strongest single image of the article. For #11, that's the family-arrival or family-meal moment. For #12, the candlelit table for two or the sunset over Taal.
