## Two long weekends, three weeks apart

August 2026 hands Filipino travelers two long weekends in a row:

- **Friday Aug 21 → Sunday Aug 23** — Ninoy Aquino Day (Friday holiday)
- **Friday Aug 28 → Monday Aug 31** — National Heroes Day (Monday holiday)

That's two 3-to-4-day windows separated by a single working week. For Manila travelers who can plan ahead, this is one of the most generous holiday months of the year — and Lipa is a smart choice for either or both. This article is the booking guide: when to leave, what's open, what to expect from August weather, and how to decide if you should book one weekend, the other, or stretch the whole period into a longer stay.

## Why Lipa for these weekends specifically

A few reasons August in Lipa is a better call than the obvious alternatives:

**Tagaytay is booked out, and surcharged.** August long weekends are when Tagaytay rates climb hardest. Lipa's pricing stays stable — no holiday surcharge.

**The weather is workable.** August is still rainy season, but the daily pattern is predictable (clear mornings, afternoon showers). Beaches are a coin-flip. Lipa is a known quantity.

**It's still 1 hour from Manila.** No three-hour Tagaytay traffic line. No Baguio overnight drive. No flight to Cebu. SLEX → STAR Tollway → Lipa.

**Inventory holds longer.** Tagaytay rentals fill three to four weeks ahead for long weekends. Lipa rentals — including [HavenInLipa](https://haveninlipa.com) — typically have availability 7 to 14 days out, even for August.

**Mt. Maculot is best in August.** The trail is greener after monsoon rain, the views are dramatic with cloud movement, and the early-morning starts are pleasantly cool. Best hiking month of the year.

## Pick your weekend: Ninoy Aquino Day vs. Heroes Day

The two long weekends play differently. Here's how to choose:

### Ninoy Aquino Day weekend (Aug 21–23)

**Length.** 3 days (Fri–Sun), or 4 if you take Thursday off.

**Vibe.** The first long weekend after the dead month of July. Lighter crowds than Heroes Day because school-aged kids may still be navigating early August academic adjustments depending on the school calendar.

**Best for.** Couples, solo travelers, smaller family groups who want a slightly quieter long weekend. Pre-schoolyear-end-rush, less competitive booking.

**Booking competition.** Moderate. Book 14 to 21 days ahead.

**Itinerary fit.** Slow weekend, 1–2 light activities, lots of cafe and food time.

### National Heroes Day weekend (Aug 28–31)

**Length.** 4 days (Fri–Mon). Heroes Day is the Monday, so the long weekend is naturally longer.

**Vibe.** The end-of-August "last big break before September school routine settles in." Higher demand because of the 4-day length.

**Best for.** Families who want maximum time, couples who want a real reset, anyone planning an extended-stay arrival or departure.

**Booking competition.** Higher. Book 21 to 28 days ahead, especially for the 2BR family-size rentals.

**Itinerary fit.** A real reset weekend — one big activity (Mt. Maculot, Taal Volcano), one heritage day, two slow days.

### The stretch option: Aug 21 through Aug 31

For travelers with maximum flexibility (remote workers, retirees, anyone who can take a single working week off), the smart play is to stay through the whole period: arrive Thu Aug 20, depart Mon Aug 31. 11 nights.

You get both long weekends plus the quiet working week between them. The cost-per-night drops with weekly rates. The "first long weekend you don't want to leave on" problem solves itself.

## What's open, what's closed

A practical August long-weekend rundown of operating hours:

**Open on holiday Fridays/Mondays:**
- HavenInLipa (24/7 access via self-check-in)
- Most family-run restaurants (Casa Marikit, Beegee's, Café de Lipa) — typically normal Friday hours, sometimes shortened
- SM City Lipa — normal mall hours
- San Sebastian Cathedral — normal daily Mass schedule, possibly with a special holiday Mass
- Lipa public market — normal hours
- Hospitals (Mary Mediatrix, Lipa Medix) — 24/7

**Possibly closed or shortened on the holiday itself:**
- Government offices (closed)
- Smaller boutique cafes (may close)
- Casa de Segunda (call ahead — may close on national holidays)
- Some museums

**Adjusted on holiday weekends generally:**
- Some restaurants run special "long weekend" menus or shortened hours
- Mt. Maculot trail tends to be busier — register early
- Toll plazas at SLEX and STAR may add staff but you'll still see traffic at peak hours

**Plan ahead:** Call your top 1–2 restaurant choices on the Wednesday before the long weekend to confirm hours. Save yourself the showing-up-to-a-closed-door problem.

## What about weather risk in late August?

Late August in Batangas is wet but not catastrophic. Expect:

- Rain on 5 to 6 days out of 10
- Most rain falls between 2 PM and 6 PM
- Mornings usually clear
- Temperature 22–28°C
- One in three Augusts has at least one typhoon affecting Luzon — usually a brief, manageable system, occasionally a serious one

**Risk management.** Don't book non-refundable flights anywhere in early September if you're planning the Aug 21–31 stretch. Build a one-day buffer on departure for typhoon weeks. **Heroes Day weekend (Aug 28–31):** Book 21 to 28 days ahead. The 2BR fills first.

## Common August long-weekend questions

**Will Lipa restaurants be crowded?**
Casa Marikit and Café de Lipa are busier than average — book ahead. Beegee's is fast-service and rarely has a long wait. Most other restaurants have manageable queues even on holiday weekends.

**Is Mt. Maculot crowded?**
Yes, on Saturday and Sunday of the long weekend. Start at 5:30–6:00 AM and you'll avoid most of it. Weekday hikes (the middle of the stretch option) are nearly empty.

**Can I do both long weekends from the same booking?**
Technically yes — stretch through the whole period. The single-trip math is the best deal of the month.

**What if I can only take one weekend, which should I pick?**
For couples: Ninoy Aquino (Aug 21–23). Quieter, slightly cheaper, less booking competition.
For families: Heroes Day (Aug 28–31). The extra day makes a big difference with kids.
For remote workers: Heroes Day. Easier to extend by a day or two.

**Are there special Independence Day-style events in August?**
Ninoy Aquino Day has limited public ceremonies (mostly in Manila around the Quirino Grandstand). National Heroes Day has wreath-laying ceremonies at military and civic memorials, less prominent in Lipa specifically. Both holidays are observed more as rest days than commemorative events for most Filipinos.

**Is August a good time to bring kids?**
Yes. Cool weather, low health risk, full restaurant and amenity availability. Some families avoid August because of rain — that's a misconception. August rain is manageable.
