## A small confession before the big news

We've been hosting in Lipa for three years now. In that time we've had hundreds of families through the 2BR Spacious and the 1BR Cozy — and one piece of feedback shows up over and over: *"This was great. Do you have anything bigger? Anything more — fun for the kids?"*

The honest answer for a long time was "not yet." We tracked every request. We kept the list. We listened.

This year we built the answer. And we're getting close to opening the doors.

## What's coming: meet Mickey in Lipa

Tucked inside Bella Vita Subdivision in Brgy. Anilao, Lipa City — a quiet residential pocket about ten minutes from the rest of the city — we've put up something different. Not another studio. Not another generic unit. A full house, designed top to bottom around the way families actually use a vacation rental.

We're calling it **Mickey in Lipa**. Two floors. Three bedrooms. One kitchen. Sleeps **up to 13 guests** across a queen master, two bunk rooms, a living-room day bed, and a flexible floor mattress — parents, kids, and the lola/lolo who decided last minute they were coming too.

And the thing that makes it land — the part the kids will not stop talking about — is the theme.

### The Disney-inspired part (yes, really)

When we designed Mickey in Lipa, we wanted a space that felt like a small adventure on its own. Something kids walk into and immediately know is *for them* — not a watered-down "kid-friendly" hotel room with a beige duvet and a fold-out cot.

So we leaned in. Mickey Mouse gallery walls. Playful murals in both kids' bedrooms. A "Dream it, Do it" Disney quote in the lounge. Ring chandeliers, modern furniture, and a Mickey-shaped accent here and there for the parents to spot. The master bedroom keeps the playful spirit but stays restful — a queen bed, soft lighting, a "all you need is a little bit of magic" wall print that you don't notice until you're already half-asleep.

It's the kind of place where check-in becomes an event. Where the kids run up the stairs to claim a bunk. Where you'll get the family photo by the gallery wall before the bags are even unpacked.

That's the bet, anyway. We'll know when the first families arrive.

## One house, three ways to book it

Here's the part most readers haven't seen done in Lipa before: Mickey in Lipa lists on the booking calendar in **three configurations** — same Disney-inspired house, three different sizes depending on the group you're traveling with. The other bedrooms simply get locked off when they're not part of your booking.

- **1-Bedroom Listing (sleeps up to 5)** — Kitchen, garage, queen master bedroom upstairs, and the living room with its day bed. Floor mattress kept on hand. Both bunk rooms (Bedroom 1 downstairs and Bedroom 2 upstairs) stay locked off. Built for couples, solo travelers, and small stays who want the full Mickey aesthetic without paying for square footage they won't use.
- **2-Bedroom Listing (sleeps up to 9)** — Everything in the 1-Bedroom, plus **Bedroom 1** downstairs (the first bunk room — two double-twin beds, sleeps 4). Bedroom 2 upstairs stays locked off. The right size for families of 4 to 9 who want a private bunk room for the kids but don't need the whole house.
- **3-Bedroom / Full House Listing (sleeps up to 13)** — The entire Mickey in Lipa. Both bunk rooms unlocked, queen master, day bed, floor mattress, every room of the house yours for the weekend. The hero booking for multi-generation families, barkadas, and birthday weekends.

| Configuration | Sleeps | Rooms included |
|---|---|---|
| 1-Bedroom | 5 | Master, living room (day bed), kitchen, garage |
| 2-Bedroom | 9 | All of the above + Bedroom 1 (GF bunk room) |
| 3-Bedroom / Full House | 13 | All of the above + Bedroom 2 (2F bunk room) |

The Disney gallery wall, the Mickey murals, the ring chandeliers, the full kitchen — those come with every configuration. What changes between listings is just how many bedrooms unlock with your stay.

For most direct-booking guests, this is a quiet upgrade: instead of being told "the unit you wanted is booked," you can scale the booking up or down depending on the group. If you've ever wanted to book HavenInLipa but the unit you wanted was unavailable on your dates — this flexibility is the answer.

Pet policy varies by unit and stay length. Message us before booking with your pet details and we'll let you know honestly whether it works for your dates.

Yes — and we'll prep the unit for it if you give us a heads-up. Welcome arrangements, decorations, late check-out, the things hosting a hotel can't really do.
