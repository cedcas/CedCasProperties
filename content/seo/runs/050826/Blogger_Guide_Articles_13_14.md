## How to Add Images in WordPress

1. Click where you want the image in your content
2. Type `/image` and press Enter
3. Click **Upload** and select your file
4. After uploading, click the image → go to **Block** tab in right sidebar → fill in **Alt Text**
5. Keep image file names lowercase with hyphens (e.g., `mickey-in-lipa-gallery-wall-lounge.jpg`)
6. For the Mickey in Lipa property renders, **add a caption underneath** noting "Render — final property may vary" until real photos are available. This protects you if the launched property differs from the design renders.

## Featured Image Setup (Both Articles)

In the WordPress Post sidebar:
1. Click **Featured Image** → **Set Featured Image**
2. Upload the featured image file
3. Fill in Alt Text (use the **Featured Image Alt Text** from each article's metadata above)
4. Save

The featured image is what shows on the blog homepage and on social shares — pick the strongest single image of the article.
