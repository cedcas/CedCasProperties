# Internal Linking Strategy & Link Matrix — HavenInLipa Ecosystem

**Owner:** Content team / blog editor
**Status:** Live ecosystem has 8 blog articles + 4 main-site pages — linking matrix is unaudited as of May 8, 2026
**Goal:** Turn the existing content + property + commercial pages into a topical authority graph that distributes PageRank, drives book-direct conversion, and earns featured snippet eligibility.

---

## Why this matters now

In 23 days HavenInLipa scaled from 2 blog articles to 8. Add the homepage, 2 property pages, the embedded Book Direct article, the Privacy/Terms pages, and the upcoming Family + Romantic articles, and the ecosystem has roughly 14 indexable URLs.

Without internal linking discipline, those pages compete with each other instead of compounding. With it, every new article becomes a vote for the property pages, and every property page funnels traffic to the book-direct conversion path.

Done right, this is one of the highest-ROI on-page activities available in May.

---

## Three linking principles

1. **Every page should pass authority forward to a conversion page.** Property pages and the Book Direct article are conversion pages. Every blog article should link to at least one of them, contextually.
2. **Link with descriptive anchor text — never "click here" or "read more."** The anchor text is a ranking signal. Use the target page's primary keyword.
3. **Link inside the body, not just at the end.** Footer "related posts" widgets carry less weight than a contextual in-paragraph link. Aim for 2 to 4 in-body links per article plus 1 to 2 footer/CTA links.

## Anchor text patterns

Use descriptive anchors that match the target page's primary keyword. Examples:

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use instead |
|---|---|
| "click here" | "see our Mt. Maculot hiking guide" |
| "read more" | "the full Book Direct vs. Airbnb breakdown" |
| "this article" | "our 2-day weekend itinerary for Lipa" |
| "learn more" | "browse the 2BR family rental in Lipa" |

Vary the anchor text across articles linking to the same page. Don't repeat the exact same anchor on every link to a property page — Google's anomaly detection flags exact-match anchor over-use.

## In-body link guidelines per article

- **Minimum:** 2 links to other blog articles + 1 link to a property page + 1 link to Book Direct = 4 contextual in-body internal links
- **Maximum:** No hard ceiling, but avoid >8 internal links per 2,000-word article (looks spammy and dilutes signal)
- **Placement:** First in-body link should appear in the first 30% of the article, ideally in the intro or first body section
- **Density:** Roughly one internal link per 400 to 500 words of body copy

Either way: in February 2027, run a Request Indexing pass via GSC URL Inspection two weeks before Holy Week to maximize the 2027 capture window.
